During the Games, a wide-area shuttle bus service will operate between major locations (Kinosaki Onsen/Kumihama, Tottori Station, Himeji Station) and the competition venues.

This service is by advance registration only, with a capacity of 90 passengers per route (first-come, first-served).

We are currently preparing to open registration on IOF Eventor. Please wait a little longer until registration opens.


## Operating Schedule and Plan Overview

* Basic Operating Days (7 days total): May 21st (Fri) - May 26th (Wed), May 28th (Fri), 2027

    There will be no service on May 27th (Thu), the training day for this wide-area bus.

* Selectable Plans:

    * Standard Plan (7 days total): Round trip between each departure/arrival point and the venue for the entire duration.
    * Himeji Transfer Plan (Kinosaki Onsen/Kumihama / Tottori Departure): The first half of the trip will be based in Kinosaki Onsen/Kumihama or Tottori, with travel to Himeji after the competition on May 26th (Wed), and a Himeji departure/arrival flight on May 28th (Fri) (a seat on the May 28th Himeji flight will also be reserved).
    * Forest-Only Plan (Himeji Departure Only): A 4-day plan for those participating only in the Forest event (May 24th-26th, 28th).

## Registration

* **How to Apply:** Registration will be accepted via IOF Eventor (registration will open as soon as preparations are complete).
* **Early Bird Deadline:** Thursday, December 31, 2026, at 23:59 (JST)
* **Final Registration Deadline:** Sunday, February 28, 2027, at 23:59 (JST)
  * *Note: Registration will close before the deadline once the capacity (90 passengers per route) is reached.*
* **Timetable:** Detailed operating schedules for each route will be announced on the official event website after the registration deadline. Buses will operate on a schedule that arrives in time for the first start (top start).

## Cancellation Policy & Refunds

* **If a seat cannot be secured due to full capacity:** A full refund of the amount paid will be issued without any fees, regardless of the reason.
* **Cancellation due to participant's convenience (until February 28, 2027, at 23:59 JST):** A refund will be issued after deducting a 10% cancellation fee from the amount paid.
* **Cancellation on or after Monday, March 1, 2027:** No refunds will be issued for cancellations made for the participant's convenience.
